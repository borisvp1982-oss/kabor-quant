What KaBor Quant is

KaBor Quant is your modular trading system for MyFundedPerps (MFP). It runs on the Raspberry Pi around the clock: each bot watches one instrument, runs one or more strategies and trades on paper or in your MFP sandbox accounts. You control everything from this panel, and with the SMT connector Claude can read your bots, review your analysis and prepare plans that you confirm.

BotOne instrument (BTC, XAU, NAS100…), a base timeframe and its risk limits.
StrategyA .py file that decides when to enter and where the stop goes. A bot can run up to 5.
Manual planYour analysis turned into rules: the bot executes it when the conditions are met.
SMTThe connector with Claude: reads bots, candles and your drawings, and proposes plans. It never trades on its own.

Sandbox only. KaBor rejects fp_live_ keys and any server other than the MFP sandbox. Nothing it does is financial advice: backtest and paper results do not guarantee future results.

Getting started

Signing in

Open the address shown by ./bot.sh panel (for example https://your-Raspberry-IP:8083) and sign in with your user. «Keep me signed in» keeps the session for 30 days on that device; otherwise it closes after 12 hours of inactivity. In Settings you can turn on two-step verification with an authenticator app.

The menu

  • Overview: all bots at a glance.
  • Tools: Statistics, Analysis, Journal, Plans, Payouts and expenses, Portfolio and Monitor.
  • Bots: every bot, grouped in folders, with its result and status (green dot: running).
  • Settings group: Accounts, Strategies, Global risk, News, Instruments and Alerts.
  • System: Settings and this Help.

At the top is the price ticker of your instruments (▲/▼ when a position is open). On desktop the menu can be collapsed; on the phone it opens with ☰. The panel can be installed as an app on your phone (Settings → Mobile app) if you connect with a valid certificate, for example through Tailscale.

Overview

At the top, today's, this week's and the total result in R, and the open trades. Below, one card per bot with its strategy, price, mini curve and status. At the bottom: the live feed, the Raspberry Pi, alerts, accounts, archived bots, the log and the ZIP report of all bots.

R is the unit used across the panel: 1 R is what you risk on a trade (from entry to stop). Making 2 R means making twice what you risked. If the bot has «Risk per trade ($)», you will also see dollars.

Bots

Creating a bot

Menu → New bot. Give it a name, pick strategy, symbol and timeframe, backtest it and press «Launch». «Start from the settings of» copies another bot's settings. From Strategies, «New bot with this one» opens it with that strategy already selected.

The bot panel

Each bot has two tabs: Dashboard (monitoring) and Settings. On the dashboard you will see:

  • Today and this week, with the bar of its daily limits.
  • Price and strategy: live chart with the strategy lines, the open trade and the «TradingView ↗» link.
  • Simulated trade and, if it trades on an account, the Accounts card with the real position.
  • Manual management of the open trade: stop to break-even, move stop or target, partial close (5–90 %) or close now. It applies to the model and to the account immediately.
  • Drift monitor, Performance by strategy (if it combines strategies), statistics, trades by side, the trades table (tap one to see its chart) and the activity log.

Bot controls

  • Pause entries: no new trades; the open one keeps its stop and target.
  • Archive: stops working and is kept with its full history. It can be reactivated from the Overview.
  • Statuses: «Looking for signals», «Trade opened», «Loading history», «Paused», «Stopped by the guardian», «Strategy error».

Bot settings

The configuration is split into tabs: the Bot tab holds what belongs to the bot (market, risk, limits, filters, execution) and there is one tab per strategy with its own settings. The number on each tab is its unsaved changes.

  • Changes are highlighted in the form and applied when you press Save and apply, without restarting the program. Each save is a numbered configuration you can load again («Saved configurations»).
  • Changing the strategy, symbol, timeframe or trend filter reloads the bot's history (and closes its trade if the market changes).
  • «Undo changes» goes back to what is saved; «Default values», to the strategy's defaults.

Bot settings reference

All common settings, with their explanation and default value. They are generated by the program itself, so they are always up to date.

Test, simulate and optimize

Backtest

In the bot settings, the card on the right tests the form values without saving them (7, 14, 30, 90 days or the whole history) with real MFP candles, fees and the same rules as live. The running bot is not touched.

  • Result: trades, win rate, total R, profit factor, drawdown, SQN, curve and trades by side.
  • The trade list: tap one to see it drawn with its stop, target and path.
  • In a bot with several strategies, the breakdown by strategy with its blocked signals.
  • Detailed breakdown: a window with what each strategy did, when it signalled and what happened to each signal (see «Several strategies in one bot»).
  • «Tests this session» keeps the latest ones to compare.

Prop firm simulator (Monte Carlo)

Below the test: it shuffles the backtest days thousands of times and applies the rules of a challenge (daily loss, max loss, target, minimum days, time limit). It tells you the probability of passing, of breaking the account, the typical days and which risk per trade suits you. «Shuffle in 5-day blocks» is stricter and more realistic.

Optimize settings (walk-forward)

Tests combinations of one or two settings and checks with walk-forward whether what works holds on data it has not seen. Load the robust zone (gold box), not an isolated star. If the verdict is «overfitted», change nothing: the strategy does not improve by tweaking numbers.

With fewer than 30 trades, a backtest says little. Always test over long and different periods.

Strategies

A strategy is a .py file in the estrategias/ folder. In Strategies you see the installed ones and which bots use each, and you can download them, upload a new one or a new version (the bots using it reload it on their own) and remove them if no bot uses them. Replaced versions are kept in estrategias/anteriores/.

Your installed strategies

Testing a file without installing it

./bot.sh probar estrategias/cruce_emas.py --simbolo BTC --tf 15m --dias 60
./bot.sh probar estrategias/cruce_emas.py --simbolo XAU --tf 5m --dias 30 --ajuste ce_rr=1.5

Writing a strategy

Only the senales() function is needed: it returns the side (1 long, −1 short) and the stop price. Target, break-even, trailing, limits and execution are handled by the core. The full explanation is in ESTRATEGIAS.md and a commented example in estrategias/cruce_emas.py. You can also ask me (Claude) to write one from your idea.

A strategy file is code that runs on the Raspberry Pi. Only upload your own files or files you trust.

Several strategies in one bot

A bot can run up to 4 strategies besides the main one, on the same instrument. You choose them in the Bot tab → Market → «More strategies in this bot». Each added strategy has its own tab with its settings and timeframe.

One trade at a time

  • All strategies watch at the same time. The first valid signal opens the trade and blocks the rest until it closes (TP, stop, that strategy's exit or manually). Opposite signals neither close nor open anything: they are counted as blocked.
  • The strategy that opened the trade owns it: its target, its break-even and its exit.
  • Risk, limits, trading hours, filters and execution belong to the bot (Bot tab).

Each strategy with its own timeframe and direction

Set the bot to the smallest timeframe and give each strategy its own (equal or larger and a multiple): for example, a 5m bot with an ORB on 5m, a Keltner breakout on 30m and a Supertrend on 4h. The bot builds each strategy's candles from its own; each one sees only its closed candles, with its own ATR. Signals enter at the open of the bot's next candle, and the stop and target are checked on every bot candle.

Direction: each strategy has its own in its tab («both», «longs only» or «shorts only»), the main one included. If one works better only on shorts, leave it on shorts only without touching the others. Signals on the other side count as filtered by «direction». Settings saved before 9.3 keep what they had: the direction that belonged to the bot goes to all its strategies.

Priority by score

If several signal on the same candle, the one with the best score enters. The score is the SQN of each strategy's backtest on its own, on its timeframe, with the bot's settings and the last 90 days: mean R ÷ standard deviation × √trades (up to 100). As a guide: above 1.6 good, above 2.5 very good; below 0, it loses. With fewer than 10 trades it has no score and goes last. It is recalculated when you save the configuration, every day and with «Recalculate score».

Performance by strategy

On the bot dashboard: bars with each strategy's R live and in its backtest, each one's curve, and a card per strategy with its priority, score, trades, win rate, profit factor, drawdown, dollars and blocked signals (the opportunities missed because of the one-trade-at-a-time rule).

Detailed breakdown of a test

After a backtest, the Detailed breakdown button opens a window with everything each strategy did in that test (also in a single-strategy bot):

  • Comparison of the strategies: timeframe, direction, trades, win rate, PF, R, average R, drawdown, SQN, signals, blocked and filtered; and the curve of each one.
  • When each strategy signals: one row per strategy with every signal (▲ long, ▼ short) and its trades. Filled: enters; grey: blocked by another strategy’s trade; orange: filtered; red: daily limit. Tap or hover a mark to see what happened. Drag to move and scroll to zoom.
  • How they get in each other’s way: which strategy blocked which, and how many times.
  • One card per strategy: longs versus shorts, what happened to its signals, who blocked it, which filters remove its signals, trades by hour and day of the week and exit reasons, with ideas to try (for example, shorts only if it wins on shorts and loses on longs).

«Download data» saves the test to a JSON file for the laptop script.

Analysing the strategies from your laptop

herramientas/analizar_estrategias.py asks the Raspberry Pi for many tests of the same bot and writes an HTML report with what is worth trying. It only needs Python 3 on the laptop (Windows, macOS or Linux), nothing else to install. Copy the file to the laptop and run it:

python analizar_estrategias.py --pi https://YOUR-PANEL-ADDRESS --usuario YOUR_USER --bot 3
python analizar_estrategias.py --pi https://... --usuario YOUR_USER --bot 3 --dias 180 --rapido
python analizar_estrategias.py --archivo desglose_BTC_15m_2026-11-25.json
  • It asks for the password (and the two-step code, if you use it) when it runs; it doesn’t store it.
  • It tests the current settings, each strategy alone, the bot without each one, each direction of each strategy and each priority order (--rapido skips the orders). The tests run on the Raspberry Pi with its candles; the bots keep running.
  • The report compares the variants with the current one (Δ R), counts the signals each strategy blocked for another and what they would have made (from the test of that strategy alone), the signals against the open trade, the timeline of the last 14 days and signals by hour. Next to the report you get senales.csv, operaciones.csv and variantes.csv for Excel. The report and the files are in Spanish.
  • With the Raspberry Pi’s own certificate: copy data/tls/cert.pem to the laptop and add --certificado cert.pem, connect through Tailscale, or, only on your home network, use --sin-verificar.
  • --archivo analyses a JSON from «Download data» without connecting (no variants).

Testing many variants and keeping the best one overfits. Only change something if it improves with plenty of trades and in another period too (for example, --dias 90 and --dias 180).

The «Manual plan» strategy always runs in its own bot: it cannot be combined with other strategies.

Manual plans and the analysis chart

A plan is your analysis turned into rules: instrument, side, entry condition, stop, optional TP1, target, invalidation and expiry. It is executed by a bot running the «Manual plan» strategy on the same instrument and timeframe. A new plan stays pending until you confirm it.

Entry conditions

  • Limit order at a price: it fills when the price touches it, without waiting for the close. On the account, KaBor watches the price live and enters at market when it is touched. It is withdrawn on its own if price reaches the target first, if the invalidation is met or if it expires.
  • A candle closes above/below a level (with N closes in a row).
  • Price rises/falls and touches a level.

TP1: closes a percentage and, if you want, moves the stop to break-even. Size: by default from the bot's risk (contracts = risk ÷ distance to the stop), or a fixed number of contracts. An active plan is cancelled with a waiting period of a few minutes, for safety.

Analysis chart

With real MFP candles. Tools:

  • Move: select and drag what you have drawn.
  • Level: entry, stop, TP1, final TP, invalidation or free level.
  • Zone: bullish or bearish FVG, order block, liquidity or free zone (dragging or tapping two corners; it can extend «until now»).
  • Trend line: a line between two points.
  • ▲ Long / ▼ Short: the position tool, as in TradingView. Green box up to the target and red box down to the stop; drag each line, the box or its right edge. Labels show price, %, $ and R:R, with contracts from the bot's risk (or the risk and contracts you type in the list).
  • Mark FVGs: marks the 6 open three-candle gaps closest to the price. The same button removes them.
  • Create plan from chart (or «Create plan» on a position): fills «Manual plan» with your levels and a summary of your zones. You review it and create it as pending.

Drawings are saved on the Raspberry Pi per instrument; live plans show as dashed lines. On the phone you draw with your finger.

SMT: Claude with your bots

SMT is an MCP connector that lets Claude read your system and propose plans. Claude never confirms, trades or cancels an active plan: you do that in the panel.

Connecting it

  1. On the Raspberry Pi: ./bot.sh conector gives you the connector's private address.
  2. In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector → paste the address.
  3. If KaBor adds new tools, disconnect and reconnect, and open a new chat.

Examples of what you can ask

  • «Review what I marked on BTC 15m and prepare the plan.»
  • «Look at this Nasdaq screenshot: I want to sell in the 1h FVG with the stop above the high. Does it match the real candles?»
  • «How are my bots doing this week? Separate my analysis from my execution.»

Analysis (TradingView)

The full TradingView chart (indicators, drawings, several timeframes) of the same market MFP uses. KaBor picks the symbol by itself: Binance → BINANCE:BTCUSDT.P, Hyperliquid (Trade[XYZ]) → HIP3XYZ:XYZ100USDC.P. Next to the symbol it shows «SAME MARKET» or «APPROXIMATE» (forex and some indices do not exist as such on TradingView). If one does not load, type another in «TradingView symbols».

There may be small differences from the MFP price: the bots always trade on MFP candles. Claude cannot read what you draw on TradingView; that is what the Plans analysis chart is for.

Monitoring

Statistics

The performance of all your bots together or of each one: R, win rate, profit factor, curve, drawdown, calendar, months, hours, days, duration, exit reasons and favourable and adverse excursion (MFE/MAE).

Journal

All trades by day. Tap one to write a note, add tags and mark whether it was according to plan. You can log trades you placed by hand. At the top you see what breaking your plan costs you.

Drift monitor (Monitor)

Compares each bot's real results with thousands of possible streaks from its backtest: «as in the backtest», «to watch», «drifting» or «few trades». It alerts on Telegram and can pause the bot when it starts drifting.

Portfolio

Puts several bots together as if they were one account: combined drawdown, correlation, risk open at the same time and the prop firm simulator with all of them together.

Payouts and expenses

Your payouts, expenses and refunds in euros (ECB rate when needed), with receipts, yearly and quarterly summaries, CSV and a printable report. Only in the panel; never through the connector. Back up data/fiscal.

Sandbox accounts and risk

Trading on the sandbox accounts

  1. In MFP → Settings → API Keys, create a Test (Sandbox) key with Read And Trade access (it starts with fp_test_).
  2. Panel → Accounts → «Add account»: name, key, «Find the accounts for this key», choose the account and turn on «Send orders».
  3. In the bot → Bot tab → Sandbox execution: mode sandbox, its accounts and the risk per trade (start low).

Before each order it checks the account, its trading policy, the daily loss left after the stop, that the price has not already passed the stop, the cost, the minimum size, the notional and the leverage. Each order carries an idempotency key (it is never duplicated) and retries respect the API limits. Keys are only shown by their last 4 characters.

Account stream and read limit

MyFundedPerps limits reads (GET) to 30 per minute per key in sandbox; placing, changing or closing orders doesn’t count. Since 9.4, KaBor opens one account stream per key: MFP pushes every change to orders, fills, positions and balance as it happens, so KaBor no longer checks the position on every candle or polls an order until it fills. An entry goes from about 12 reads to 1–3, and a close to none.

  • Each account shows «Account stream» (connected or reconnecting) and «Reads per minute» (now, maximum in the last hour and in 24 hours).
  • KaBor stays at 24 reads per minute per key at most and leaves room for trading: the panel’s periodic read waits if the key is busy.
  • If the stream drops, it reconnects on its own and, meanwhile, KaBor asks the API as before.
  • If MFP warns that the key is over its limit, or throttles it with a 429, it goes to the log and you get a «problems» Telegram alert (at most one per hour); KaBor waits as long as MFP asks before reading again.

Global risk

Limits above those of each bot: daily and weekly loss and profit of the whole set and trades open at the same time. When they are reached, entries stop on all bots.

News

No entries around economic calendar news (Forex Factory): by currency and impact, with wider windows for the big ones (FOMC, NFP, CPI…). Open trades are not touched. It comes turned off.

Instruments, alerts and settings

Instruments

The MFP markets your bots use and their fees. Search and add more from the catalogue. If MFP moves a market (like the Nasdaq, which moved to composite|XYZ100), KaBor finds it by name, updates itself and lets you know.

Telegram alerts

  1. In Telegram, talk to @BotFather, type /newbot and copy the token.
  2. Panel → Alerts: paste the token, «Check», send /start to your bot and «Detect my chat».
  3. Choose what to be alerted about (signal, open, close, limits, problems, daily summary…) and send a test message.

Bots work with closed candles: the signal is sent when the candle that gives it closes. After a restart, alerts from recovered candles arrive marked «late».

Settings

Your password, two-step verification, open sessions, language (Spanish, English or French), the price ticker, themes and the phone app.

Updating with a backup

Leave the new version's zip in your home folder (~), not inside ~/kcbot2:

cd ~/kcbot2 && ./bot.sh parar
cd ~ && tar czf kcbot2_copia_$(date +%Y%m%d_%H%M).tgz --exclude=venv kcbot2
unzip -o ~/kabor_X.Y_raspberry.zip -d ~/kcbot2
cd ~/kcbot2 && ./bot.sh arrancar

Then reload the panel with Ctrl+F5. To go back:

cd ~/kcbot2 && ./bot.sh parar
cd ~ && mv kcbot2 kcbot2_descartada
tar xzf kcbot2_copia_YYYYMMDD_HHMM.tgz
mv kcbot2_descartada/venv kcbot2/
cd ~/kcbot2 && ./bot.sh arrancar

Where everything is stored

  • estrategias/: the strategy files.
  • data/bots.json and data/bots/<n>/: each bot with its configuration, state, operaciones.csv, ejecuciones.csv, events, candles and reports.
  • data/cuentas.json (keys), data/usuarios.json, data/sesiones.json and data/tls/: private, readable only by your user. Do not share them.
  • data/dibujos.json, data/planes.json, data/diario.json, data/fiscal/, data/avisos.json, data/instrumentos.json.

Frequent problems

«MyFundedPerps read limit» alert
A key went over 30 reads per minute. Check in Accounts that the stream is connected; if not, look at ./bot.sh logs. Use each key only in KaBor: another program with the same key adds reads to the same limit.
A bot stays in «Loading history»
Check ./bot.sh logs. If it says the market no longer appears in the catalogue, MFP has moved it: since 9.1 KaBor looks for it on its own; if it cannot find it, check the instrument in Instruments. If it is a network error, wait: the bot retries on its own.
«Problems» alerts about DNS or network (Errno -3)
The Raspberry Pi briefly lost network or DNS. If it happens often, check the WiFi or set a fixed DNS on the router (1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8).
«Strategy error»
The log shows the line of the file that fails. The bot carries on with the next candle. Fix the file and upload it again in Strategies.
The browser warns that the connection is not private
It is the Raspberry Pi's own certificate: the connection is encrypted. Accept the warning once, or connect through Tailscale to get a valid certificate.
I can't combine two strategies
«Manual plan» cannot be combined. The others can (up to 4 besides the main one); their timeframe must be equal to or larger than the bot's and a multiple of it.
Claude doesn't see a new SMT tool
In Claude, disconnect and reconnect the connector and open a new chat.
Something appears in Spanish with the panel in English or French
That phrase is missing from the dictionary: send a screenshot and it will be added.
